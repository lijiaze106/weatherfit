About WeatherFit

What it is

WeatherFit helps you choose a sport that fits today's weather. You tell it the place, the weather and the temperature, and it suggests sports and a gear checklist.

Who it is for

Students and beginners who exercise outdoors and often wonder: "Can I do my sport today, and what should I bring?"

How it was built

The site is made with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no server, no database and no account. The weather is typed in by hand, and the suggestions come from a list of sports and simple rules.

Please note

The suggestions are general guidance. Use your own judgement about local conditions and what your body can handle.
