About WeatherFit

A small website that helps you choose a sport that fits today's weather.

User stories

A user story says who needs something, what they want and why. These are the stories WeatherFit was built around, and each one links to the page that delivers it.

Main story

As a student who exercises outdoors,

I want to enter today's place, weather and temperature,

so that I know which sports fit and what gear to bring.

It works when

  • I can choose a place and a weather and type a temperature.
  • I get one clear verdict (go outside, go with gear, or stay indoors) and one best sport with reasons.
  • I get a gear checklist for that sport that I can tick off.
  • I can save the plan as a PDF or copy it as text.
  • My plan is saved under my name, and I can look at my history later.
  • If something is missing or wrong, the page tells me what to fix.

What it is

WeatherFit helps you choose a sport that fits today's weather. You tell it the place, the weather and the temperature, and it gives you one clear verdict, your best sport, a gear checklist and a report you can save as a PDF.

Who it is for

Students and beginners who exercise outdoors and often wonder: "Can I do my sport today, and what should I bring?"

How it was built

The site is made with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no server and no account. The history is saved in your own browser (localStorage), so it stays on your device. You can choose the weather by hand or load it for your location from Open-Meteo. The recommendation comes from a list of sports and a simple scoring rule, and the PDF report uses the browser's print function.

Please note

The suggestions are general guidance. Use your own judgement about local conditions and what your body can handle. The name you type is only saved in this browser, so please do not enter private information.
